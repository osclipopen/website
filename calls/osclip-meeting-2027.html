OSCLIP Second In-person Meeting

Published November 3, 2026

The 2nd OSCLIP In-Person Meeting at UC Berkeley

Please join us for the 2nd OSCLIP In-Person Meeting on the campus of University of California, Berkeley, **January 13-14th, 2027**.

As a grassroots community of library and information professionals with shared interest and passion for open science, the in-person gathering is our space to get together, learn about each other's work, develop ideas collaboratively, and form a deeper connection.

At this year’s meeting, we will learn more about the exciting community-led projects that grew directly out of the last in-person gathering, hear from a featured speaker, Kirstie Whitaker (Berkeley Institute for Data Science), on building and sustaining thriving open science communities, and have plenty of built-in time to connect through hands-on activities and small group discussions.

Co-Located Event & Travel Support

This year, we are excited to team up with the Open Science Primer, taking place on January 12–13 at the same location. Travel stipends are available through the support of the Lyrasis Catalyst Fund for up to $1,200 (max 8 people). The Open Science Primer has travel stipends of up to $2,000 available for Primer attendees. If you are attending both events and are already supported by the Primer, you are eligible for $500 in addition through OSCLIP.
