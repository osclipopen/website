Call for application for OSCLIP Funding Opportunities & Travel support for 2nd In-Person Gathering

We invite all OSCLIP participants, contributors, and other library and information professionals to apply for upcoming funding opportunities and join us for our next in-person meeting! These awards, supported by the Lyrasis Catalyst Fund, are designed to help pilot project outcomes at your institution, share our work at professional conferences, and connect with peers in person. You are welcome to apply for multiple opportunities. You don’t need to be a project team member to apply, but they will be given first consideration.

Deadline for Pilot Implementation Stipends: October 30, 2026
Deadline for Project Dissemination Stipends: Rolling application

1. Pilot Implementation Stipends

  • Overview: Build on the momentum of our on-going projects, and to test the suitability of project outcomes for broader adoption, the Pilot Implementation Stipends support OSCLIP members in testing one of our four project prototypes at their home institutions. The four projects are: 1) Resource knowledge base; 2) Case study templates; 3) Instructional resources; 4) Open science key competencies. Find more details about the projects on our OSF repository.
  • Funding & Duration: A total of 8 stipends of $1,000 each will be awarded to individuals for their time and effort. The testing period runs for 6 months (November 2026 – April 2027). The selection process will prioritize even distribution across projects.
  • What’s Involved: Testers will pilot a prototype (product created from a project), collect qualitative and quantitative feedback on usability and outcomes when possible, and submit a brief written summary report at the end of the project period. Your insights will directly address implementation friction, refine final outputs, and help shape turn-key toolkits for wide adoption across academic libraries. Examples of such a pilot may include: adopting materials from instructional resources repository or resources from the knowledge base; using case study templates to solicit and collect successful examples of open science initiatives; or using the open science key competencies list to improve cross-functional collaboration or professional development.
  • Deliverable: Written summary report should be submitted to OSCLIP
  • Deadline: October 30, 2026
  • How to apply: Fill out this Application Form

2. Travel Stipends for OSCLIP Project Dissemination

  • Overview: Help increase project visibility and encourage broad community adoption by sharing our findings on a national or regional stage.
  • Funding & Eligibility: 5 travel stipends of $1,500 each are available to offset travel costs for presenting OSCLIP projects at relevant professional conferences. This opportunity is open to OSCLIP project contributors or pilot facilitators. Presentations delivered before 7/28/2026 are not eligible.
  • What’s Involved: Present OSCLIP project outcomes at a relevant professional conference within the award period (7/28/2026 - 7/31/2027). All presentation formats that are suitable to showcase the project’s process, outcome, or impact are acceptable, such as a poster, panel, oral presentation, or workshop. A proof of presentation/acceptance must be submitted no later than May 31, 2027.
  • Deliverable: A digital copy of the presentation should be deposited in OSCLIP’s repository (TBD) with an open license.
  • Deadline: Rolling application
  • How to apply: Fill out this Application Form

3. Travel Stipends for the 2nd OSCLIP In-Person Meeting at UC Berkeley

  • Overview: Join us on Thursday, January 14, 2027, hosted at the University of California, Berkeley, with a welcome reception on the evening of Wednesday, January 13. Together, we will reconnect, share project outcomes, plan pilot testing and dissemination strategies, and run a Design Thinking sprint to ideate our next wave of collaborative projects.
  • Co-Located Event: We are once again partnering with the Open Science Primer, taking place right before our event on January 12–13 at the same location.
  • Travel Support Options:
    • OSCLIP Meeting Only: Travel stipends of up to $1,200 each are available through the Lyrasis Catalyst Fund for up to 8 participants.
    • Open Science Primer Only: The Primer provides separate travel stipends of up to $2,000 for Primer attendees.
    • Attending Both Events: If you are attending both events and already receiving travel support through the Primer, you are eligible for an additional $500 OSCLIP stipend to cover the extra day.
  • Deadline: Apply by December 18, 2026 for funding support
  • How to apply: Register and apply for travel funding to the OSCLIP In-Person Meeting by filling out this Form.
